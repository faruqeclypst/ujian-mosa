Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026  •  Versi Bahasa Indonesia

This Privacy Policy explains how EXAMKU ("we", "the app", "the platform") collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal information of users ("you", "users") when using our services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 User-Provided Data

  • Admin/Teacher Account Data: Full name, email, password (encrypted), phone number (optional), and role within the institution.
  • Student Data: Full name, student ID (NISN/NIS), class, and gender. This data is entered by the institution's admin/teachers.
  • Institution Data: School name, logo, and system configuration.
  • Exam Content: Question text, images, formulas, and answer choices created by teachers.

1.2 Automatically Collected Data

  • Exam Data: Student answers, time spent, scores, and completion status.
  • Security Data: Logs of suspicious activity (focus loss, tab switching) for anti-cheating purposes.
  • Technical Data: Device type, browser/app version, and IP address (for access security).

1.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • GPS location
  • Phone contacts or contact lists
  • Photos or media from the device gallery (except those explicitly uploaded for exam questions)
  • Financial or credit card data

1.4 Android App Permissions

The EXAMKU Android app requests the following device permissions, used only for the purposes described:

  • Internet: Connects the app to your institution's exam server.
  • Camera: Scans QR codes or tokens to join an exam session (only active when you use the scan feature).
  • Vibrate: Provides haptic feedback for important notifications such as exam time warnings.
  • Audio Settings: Adjusts the device audio mode during exams (e.g., silencing notifications).

We do not access your microphone, GPS location, contact list, or personal files on your device.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  1. Provide and operate the computer-based testing (CBT) service.
  2. Manage user accounts and authentication.
  3. Generate grade reports and item analysis.
  4. Detect and prevent cheating during exams.
  5. Improve service quality and fix bugs.
  6. Process questions through third-party AI services (if the AI feature is enabled by an admin).

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored on servers managed by each institution (self-hosted) or designated cloud servers.
  • Passwords are encrypted using secure hashing algorithms (bcrypt).
  • Communication between the app and server is protected with HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Data access is restricted by role (admin, teacher, student) with layered authorization.

4. Sharing Data with Third Parties

We do not sell user data to third parties. Data may only be shared under the following conditions:

  • AI Services (Optional): If the AI Generator feature is enabled, question/material text is sent to an AI provider (Groq, OpenRouter, or another admin-configured provider) for processing. This data is not permanently stored by the AI provider.
  • Image Storage: Question images may be stored in cloud storage (Cloudflare R2 or similar) configured by the admin.
  • Legal Obligations: If required by applicable law or court order.

5. User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • Access: View personal data stored in the system.
  • Rectify: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Delete: Request deletion of personal data (through the institution's admin).
  • Export: Download grade data in Excel/JSON format.

6. Data Deletion

Users or institution admins can request data deletion as follows:

  • Admins can delete student data, questions, and exam results directly through the admin panel.
  • To delete an entire institution account, send an email to alfaruqasri@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request", including the institution name and account username. Requests will be processed within 30 business days.
  • Deleted data cannot be recovered.

7. Children's Protection

EXAMKU is used in formal education contexts. Data of underage students is managed by educational institutions (schools) responsible for parental/guardian consent in accordance with applicable regulations.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced through in-app notifications. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
